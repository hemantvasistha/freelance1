How it works

  1. Keep billing in your sheet. One tab, one row per invoice — the same columns you use today (16 fixed columns are documented before setup).
  2. Set the row to READY_TO_BILL and run the menu. A menu item in your own sheet sends that row to SheetFlow.
  3. Get a PDF and a payment link. The verified total, the payment link and a signed PDF link are written back into the row. The invoice is emailed to your customer.
  4. Let the reminders run. A courtesy note before the due date, a due-date note, and an overdue note after — each sent once per stage, recorded in an audit log.
  5. See payments land. When a payment is confirmed, the row is updated to PAID with the date and reference. You get a Monday summary.

What it does not do

Being explicit here saves us both a wasted call.
  • No WhatsApp reminders. Email and the sheet only.
  • No automatic bank matching. UPI payments are confirmed manually or through your payment gateway's webhook.
  • No Tally export, no accounting entries. It invoices and chases; it is not bookkeeping software.
  • Not a statutory GST filing tool. Tax is split correctly for intra-state (CGST+SGST) and inter-state (IGST) at one rate per invoice. It does not handle HSN codes, credit notes or composition schemes.
  • Not a multi-user platform. One business per workspace, and your data stays in your own Google Sheet.
